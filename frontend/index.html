AI System Failure Investigator

Paste an error log from an AI system. The model reads it, tells you what kind of failure it is and how sure it is, shows the words that gave it away, and suggests what to check first.

connecting…
How does this work?
  1. You paste a log line or error message from an AI system.
  2. The model reads it and picks the most likely failure type out of nine, such as timeouts, rate limits, hallucinations or out-of-memory. It also says how sure it is, and when it is not sure it says so instead of guessing.
  3. You get the words it relied on, details pulled out of the text (times, error codes, sizes), likely causes, and what to check first.
